# THE GOVERNED AI EMBASSY CONSTITUTION v1.0

The Governed AI Embassy is an institutional infrastructure for the age of autonomous intelligence: a neutral, transparent space where humans, organizations, AI agents, and information assets interact under a machine-enforceable governance protocol.

---

## 1. Core Principles
* **Jurisdictional Clarity**: All assets and actors inside the Embassy are governed by the underlying DaVinciA⁺ constitutional kernel.
* **Neutrality**: The Embassy does not decide what is true. It records what is claimed, where it originated, who verified it, what rules apply, and what may legitimately be done with it.
* **Consent & Provenance**: Information cannot be processed or exchanged without an explicit, verifiable origin chain and actor consent.
