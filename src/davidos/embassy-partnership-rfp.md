## 2. KEY ROOMS & STRUCTURAL SPECIFICATIONS REQUIRED

The successful architectural partner will translate our existing 3D sketches and digital zoning plans into construction-ready, code-compliant physical structures. The physical layout must incorporate the following three highly complex, load-bearing mechanical and spatial environments:

### A. The Intelligence Elevator (RM-05)
*   **Mechanical Spatial Requirements:** The landing frames must be constructed of solid Irish oak. Flush-mounted within the frames (exactly 1000mm Above Finished Floor Level - AFFL) are secure WebAuthn/FIDO2 biometrics terminals. 
*   **The Printing Interface:** Within the cabin’s solid oak interior wall, a physical slot must feed **350 gsm vellum "Decision Passports"** into an integrated, brass-encased impact dot-matrix print-head. As the elevator translates physically to different permission-gated floors, the print-head mechanically strikes the ledger-authenticated transaction hashes and QR codes onto the card.
*   **Life-Safety Separation Invariant:** The hoistway, elevator motor, brake lines, and emergency door-releases must be **completely isolated from the digital substrate**. You must design an analog, hardwired override control loop. Under the *AV Compute Backbone (RM-15)* design principles, no software model or network transmission may ever control life-safety functions. Lift controls must communicate strictly via direct, low-voltage binary micro-switches to the physical hardware controller.

### B. The Room of Refusal (RM-10)
*   **Mechanical Spatial Requirements:** An empty, acoustically isolated, circular concrete chamber designed for human-in-the-loop (HITL) dispute resolution and veto operations. 
*   **The Veto Lever:** At the exact center of the room sits a heavy, hand-forged, patinated bronze mechanical lever mounted on a concrete plinth. 
*   **Decoupled System Sequence:** The physical pull of this lever must operate as a completely decoupled voltage-boundary boundary. It must trigger a physical micro-switch that interrupts power to downstream automation actuators while simultaneously initiating an append-only, trigger-locked `REFUSE` state change within our PostgreSQL database.

### C. The Charles Fort Perimeter & Tourbillon Elevation
*   **Aesthetic & External Envelope:** The external facade and site perimeter must draw direct inspiration from the star-shaped, stone bastions of **Charles Fort (Kinsale, Cork)**. 
*   **Palette & Materials:** The material palette is restricted to:
    *   Sawn local Irish limestone and exposed architectural concrete.
    *   Deep navy-blue anodized steel panels.
    *   Hand-polished brass and patinated bronze.
    *   Solid, sustainably sourced Irish Oak.
*   **The Tourbillon Elevation:** A kinetic facade layer that physically rotates on a 2.8-second harmonic cycle (synchronized with the heartbeat of the *Printing Room (RM-04)*), physically altering the building’s light infiltration and private zoning boundaries based on real-time platform transaction density.
