### Flagship Course Topography

1. **St Andrews Links (The Old Course) [UK-SCO-STA-01]**
   - **Established:** 1552 | **Architects:** Nature / Old Tom Morris / Daw Anderson
   - **Par:** 72 | **Yardage:** 7,297 yards
   - **Routing:** Out-and-back loop with 7 shared double greens.
   - **Key Features:** Swilcan Bridge (Hole 18), Road Hole 17th (Hotel tee carry + Road Bunker).
   - **Atmospheric Profile (Fife Coastal Gale):** Elevation 5.0m MSL, Air Temp 12°C, Density $\mathbf{\rho = 1.232\text{ kg/m}^3}$.

2. **Carnoustie Golf Links (Championship Course) [UK-SCO-CAR-02]**
   - **Established:** 1842 | **Architects:** Allan Robertson / Old Tom Morris / James Braid
   - **Par:** 71 | **Yardage:** 7,421 yards
   - **Key Features:** Barry Burn 18th (tidal hazard), Spectacle Bunkers 14th.
   - **Atmospheric Profile (Angus Easterly Blast):** Elevation 8.0m MSL, Air Temp 11°C, Density $\mathbf{\rho = 1.236\text{ kg/m}^3}$.

3. **Muirfield (HCEG) [UK-SCO-MUI-03]**
   - **Established:** 1744 | **Architects:** Old Tom Morris / Harry Colt
   - **Par:** 71 | **Yardage:** 7,245 yards
   - **Routing:** Concentric loops (Outer loop clockwise, Inner loop counter-clockwise), causing wind direction to shift on virtually every single hole.
